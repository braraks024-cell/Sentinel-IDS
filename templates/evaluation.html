How to read these metrics

Precision measures how often predicted attacks are attacks; recall measures how many actual attacks were detected. F1 balances the two. Confusion matrix rows are actual labels and columns are predicted labels. Dense layers learn combinations of features; dropout reduces overfitting during training.
